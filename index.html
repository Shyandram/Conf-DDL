Calendar

Subscribe to the deadlines

A subscribed calendar keeps itself up to date: when a date is added or corrected here, your calendar app picks it up on its next refresh (Apple and Outlook: every few hours; Google: within about a day). Deadlines come with reminders 7 days and 1 day before.

My conferences

Sync stars with another device

Scan this with your phone, or open the link on the other device. Both then keep the same ★ conferences (and the same “My conferences” calendar): starring on one shows up on the other the next time it is opened.

Anyone with this link can change your stars, so share it only with your own devices.
